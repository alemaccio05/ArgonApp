Privacy Policy

Application: INK TOWER: Climb & Stick | Package: com.alemaccio.inktower | Developer: AleMaccioDev

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AleMaccioDev ("we", "our", or "us") handles user data and protects your privacy when you use our mobile application INK TOWER: Climb & Stick (Application ID: com.alemaccio.inktower).

Key Summary: INK TOWER is designed to respect your privacy completely. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal or sensitive user data. The game operates 100% offline with all progression saved strictly on your local device.

1. Types of Data Collected and Local Storage

  • No Personal or Sensitive Data: The application does not require user registration, account creation, or login of any kind. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location data, financial information, or persistent device identifiers.
  • Local Device Storage Only: All gameplay progress (such as highest reached meters, current level, bounce records, unlocked cosmetic skins, profile avatars, custom in-game nickname, audio preferences, and visual color palette) is stored strictly in your device's local memory using standard operating system local storage (Android SharedPreferences). No gameplay data is ever transmitted to proprietary or remote developer servers.

2. Offline Gameplay and Deterministic Simulated Rivals (Bots)

The game is fully functional offline without an active network connection. In-game leaderboards and daily challenges (such as "Today's Tower") utilize deterministic mathematical algorithms and procedurally generated simulated rivals (bots) based on the UTC calendar date. No user data from you or any other human player is shared or received over the network.

3. Device Permissions

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively for standard Flutter engine operations and to support optional user-initiated sharing of the game score summary (via the operating system's native share sheet). The app does not transmit background telemetry or tracking logs.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

In the current version of the application, there are no active third-party tracking, analytical, or profiling advertising SDKs. If certified ad networks (such as Google AdMob) or in-app purchases (Google Play Billing) are integrated in future updates, this policy will be promptly revised to disclose the relevant third-party providers and compliant consent mechanisms (e.g., Google UMP for EU/EEA/UK users) will be provided.

5. Data Retention, Storage, and Deletion Rights

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device, you maintain full control over your data at all times. Clearing application storage/cache via your device's Android System Settings or uninstalling the application from your device will instantly, permanently, and irrevocably delete all saved progress and user preferences.

6. Children's Privacy

The application is designed for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Questa informativa sulla privacy descrive le modalità di gestione dei dati e di tutela della privacy da parte di AleMaccioDev per l'applicazione mobile INK TOWER: Climb & Stick (ID Pacchetto: com.alemaccio.inktower).

Sintesi essenziale: INK TOWER è progettato per rispettare pienamente la tua privacy. Non raccogliamo, trasmettiamo né condividiamo alcun dato personale o sensibile. Il gioco funziona al 100% offline con salvataggi custoditi unicamente sul tuo dispositivo.

1. Tipologia di dati raccolti e archiviazione locale

  • Nessun dato personale o sensibile raccolto: L'applicazione non richiede alcuna registrazione né la creazione di un account. Non vengono raccolti nomi, indirizzi email, numeri telefonici, rubrica contatti, posizione geografica precisa o identificatori persistenti del dispositivo.

Il gioco è progettato per funzionare completamente offline senza connessione Internet.
